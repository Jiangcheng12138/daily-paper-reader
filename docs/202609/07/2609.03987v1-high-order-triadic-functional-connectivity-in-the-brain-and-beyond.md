## 摘要
在此，我们报道高阶功能网络连接作为研究大脑连接组的一种有前景的方法。传统的功能连接方法仅捕捉大脑区域之间的成对关系，忽视了支撑认知和行为的复杂多变量依赖性。首先，我们证明了高阶交互捕获更多信息，并能区分静息态和任务态的大脑活动。其次，我们引入一种基于矩阵的熵函数方法来估计三元交互，即大脑区域三元组之间的统计依赖性，并将其应用于大规模功能脑网络。由此产生的三元网络揭示了不同于传统成对功能连接分析所观察到的独特社区结构，并同时捕获了额外的连接信息。尽管三元配置可能存在组合爆炸，但网络展现出受限且分层级的结构，使得计算和解释成为可能。这些发现将三元连接定位为探测脑网络组织和高阶神经交互的有前景的下一代功能连接框架，同时强调了需要仔细考虑的关键生物学和技术挑战。

## Abstract
Here, we report high-order functional network connectivity as a promising way for studying the brain connectome. Traditional functional connectivity approaches capture only pairwise relationships between brain regions, overlooking complex multivariate dependencies that underlie cognition and behavior. First, we demonstrated that high-order interactions capture more information and can distinguish between resting-state and task-state brain activity. Second, we introduce a matrix-based entropy-functional method for estimating triadic interactions, which are statistical dependencies among triplets of brain regions, and apply it to large-scale functional brain networks. The resulting triadic networks revealed distinct community patterns that complement those observed in traditional pairwise functional connectivity analyses and simultaneously capture additional connection information. Despite the potential combinatorial explosion of triadic configurations, the networks exhibited constrained and hierarchical structures that allowed computation and interpretation. These findings position triadic connectivity as a promising next-step functional connectivity framework for probing brain network organization and high-order neural interactions, while also highlighting key biological and technical challenges that require careful consideration.
