## 摘要
与心脏和呼吸活动相关的准周期性压力脉冲在人类脑室中产生往复的脑脊液（CSF）运动。在清醒状态下，这种振荡流体运动较弱且更有规律，而最近发现，在非快速眼动（NREM）睡眠期间，这种运动明显增强，表现为连续脉冲序列中的大幅冲洗。本研究探讨这种强迫是否能诱导通过人类脑室的更大残余流动。在清醒和睡眠相关流动脉冲条件下，对第三脑室进行了瞬态CFD模拟。睡眠相关强迫产生了明显的尾侧和嘴侧优先路径，且腔内整体再循环减少。相对于每搏输出量的流动通量从约\(0.540\%\)的低值增加到睡眠期间的\(28.4\%\)。因此，睡眠相关强迫不仅改变了往复流体位移的幅度，还改变了残余流动的相对贡献。这些发现证明了第三脑室流体动力学组织的差异，并强调了这种组织对脑室内液体交换的重要性。

## Abstract
Quasi-periodic pressure pulses associated with cardiac and respiratory activity generate reciprocating cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) motion in the human ventricles. While weak and more regular during wakefulness, oscillatory fluid motions have recently been found to be much more exaggerated during NREM sleep, appearing as large flushes in successive pulse trains. The present study investigates whether this forcing can induce greater residual streaming through the human ventricles. Transient CFD simulations were performed in the third ventricle under awake and sleep-related flow pulses. Sleep-related forcing produced clear caudal and rostral preferential pathways and less overall recirculation in the cavity. The streaming flux relative to the stroke volume increased from a low value of about \(0.540\%\) to \(28.4\%\) during sleep. Thus, sleep-related forcing changes not only the magnitude of reciprocating fluid displacement but also the relative contribution of residual streaming. These findings demonstrate differences in hydrodynamic organisation in the third ventricle and highlight the importance of this organisation for intraventricular fluid exchange.
