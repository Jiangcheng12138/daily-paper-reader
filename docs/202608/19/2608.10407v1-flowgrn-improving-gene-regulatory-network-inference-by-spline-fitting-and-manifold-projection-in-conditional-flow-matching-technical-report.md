## 摘要
基因调控网络（GRNs）是理解发育和疾病过程中细胞动态及其潜在机制的基础。尽管单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）技术已能够在单细胞分辨率下收集大量的基因表达谱，但由于高维度和数据丢失，从scRNA-seq数据推断GRNs仍然是一个重大挑战。近期，FlowGRN通过应用条件流匹配（CFM）学习细胞动态，在重建细胞轨迹和推断GRNs方面展示了有前景的结果。然而，FlowGRN在重建动态的时间连贯性方面仍存在局限，并且依赖人工检查，这阻碍了下游应用和可重复性。在本文中，我们提出了FlowGRN+，这是FlowGRN的改进版本，它将样条拟合集成到CFM框架中，以生成更稳定的参考轨迹用于训练，从而改善学习动态的时间连贯性。为了解决样条拟合中的过冲问题，我们进一步引入了一种投影方案，将样条切线投影到数据流形的局部切空间上。我们在BEELINE基准上评估了FlowGRN+，显示出改进的轨迹平滑性以及具有竞争力的GRN推断性能。FlowGRN+为从scRNA-seq数据重建细胞轨迹和推断GRNs提供了一个实用框架，本工作的见解也可能对其他基于CFM的细胞动态模型有所裨益。

## Abstract
Gene regulatory networks (GRNs) are fundamental in understanding cellular dynamics and underlying mechanisms during development and disease. Although scRNA-seq technologies have enabled the collection of vast numbers of gene expression profiles at single-cell resolution, inferring GRNs from scRNA-seq data remains a significant challenge due to high dimensionality and dropout. Recently, FlowGRN has shown promising results in reconstructing cell trajectories and inferring GRNs by applying conditional flow matching (CFM) to learn the cell dynamics. However, FlowGRN still faces limitations in the temporal coherence of reconstructed dynamics and relies on human inspection, which hinders downstream applications and reproducibility. In this paper, we propose FlowGRN+, an improved version of FlowGRN that integrates spline fitting into the CFM framework to generate more stable reference trajectories for training, thereby improving the temporal coherence of the learned dynamics. To address overshooting in spline fitting, we further introduce a projection scheme that projects spline tangents onto the local tangent space of the data manifold. We evaluate FlowGRN+ on the BEELINE benchmark and show improved trajectory smoothness with a competitive GRN inference performance. FlowGRN+ provides a practical framework for reconstructing cell trajectories and inferring GRNs from scRNA-seq data, and the insights from this work may also be useful for other CFM-based models of cellular dynamics.
