## 摘要
清醒认知通常归因于觉醒系统和持续的电活动，但这些并不能解释皮层处理如何与先前情境和记忆形成相耦合。联想性突触可塑性为此类耦合提供了细胞机制，但被视为与记忆相关，而非认知的在线需求。在这里，我们开发了SynC，一种由A/C激活的化学遗传学干扰手段，能够选择性地、可逆地阻断联想性树突棘增大，同时不影响非联想性树突棘动力学、兴奋性、突触传递和NMDA受体介导的反应。在广泛表达SynC的额顶叶小鼠中，急性A/C处理可逆地损害了旷场中心探索，消除了激光点追逐，并延迟了进食启动。在安静清醒的静止期，皮层放电率和γ频段功率得以保留，但成对尖峰相关性降低。小鼠还间歇性地表现出突然的行为停滞，γ活动减少，但没有慢波睡眠中显著的δ波升高。关键的是，体内双光子谷氨酸点释放在约24%的新皮层树突棘中诱导了秒级的联想性树突棘增大，而SynC-A/C可急性阻断这一过程。这些由A/C引起的行为、回路和棘增大效应在1小时内恢复。因此，类似清醒状态的放电和γ活动不足以维持清醒认知功能。快速联想性树突棘增大对于维持功能性皮层耦合和稳定清醒是必需的。

## Abstract
Awake cognition is usually attributed to arousal systems and ongoing electrical activity, yet these do not explain how cortical processing is coupled to prior context and memory formation. Associative synaptic plasticity provides a cellular mechanism for such coupling, but is viewed as memory-related rather than an online requirement for cognition. Here we developed SynC, an A/C-activated chemogenetic perturbation that selectively and reversibly blocks associative spine enlargement while sparing non-associative spine dynamics, excitability, synaptic transmission and NMDA receptor-mediated responses. In mice with broad frontoparietal SynC expression, acute A/C reversibly impaired open-field centre exploration, abolished laser-dot chasing, and delayed feeding initiation. During quiet-wake immobile epochs, cortical firing rates and {gamma}-band power were preserved, but pairwise spike correlations were reduced. Mice also intermittently showed sudden behavioural arrest with reduced {gamma} activity but without the robust {delta} elevation of slow-wave sleep. Critically, in vivo two-photon glutamate uncaging induced seconds-scale associative spine enlargement in ~24% of neocortical spines, acutely blocked by SynC-A/C. These A/C-induced behavioural, circuit and spine-enlargement effects recovered within 1 h. Thus, wake-like firing and {gamma} activity are not sufficient to sustain awake cognitive function. Rapid associative spine enlargement is required to maintain functional cortical coupling and stable wakefulness.
