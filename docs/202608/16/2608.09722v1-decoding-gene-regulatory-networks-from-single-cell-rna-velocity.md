## 摘要
单细胞RNA测序提供了细胞状态的快照，而RNA速度则提供了其时间演化的方向信息。一个基本问题是，控制细胞动力学的隐藏基因调控网络能否从这种不完整的观测中恢复。在本研究中，我们通过将问题表述为耦合转录-剪接动力学产生的稀疏动态逆问题，开发了一个从RNA速度数据重建调控网络的数学框架。我们表明，被动快照观测通常不足以唯一识别调控相互作用，揭示了未扰动测量的内在局限性。然后我们证明，受控扰动通过生成信息丰富的调控轨迹恢复可识别性，并刻画了唯一恢复的条件。为了解决噪声和不完美观测，我们引入了一种避免数值微分的积分重建方法，并为稀疏网络恢复建立了有限样本保证。我们的结果为理解何时以及如何从单细胞观测中解码基因调控网络提供了数学基础，将扰动设计、逆问题理论和细胞反应的预测建模联系起来。

## Abstract
Single-cell RNA sequencing provides snapshots of cellular states, while RNA velocity offers directional information about their temporal evolution. A fundamental question is whether hidden gene regulatory networks governing cellular dynamics can be recovered from such incomplete observations. In this work, we develop a mathematical framework for regulatory network reconstruction from RNA velocity data by formulating the problem as a sparse dynamical inverse problem arising from coupled transcription-splicing dynamics. We show that passive snapshot observations are generally insufficient to uniquely identify regulatory interactions, revealing an intrinsic limitation of unperturbed measurements. We then prove that controlled perturbations restore identifiability by generating informative regulatory trajectories and characterize conditions for unique recovery. To address noisy and imperfect observations, we introduce an integral reconstruction approach that avoids numerical differentiation and establish finite-sample guarantees for sparse network recovery. Our results provide a mathematical foundation for understanding when and how gene regulatory networks can be decoded from single-cell observations, linking perturbation design, inverse problem theory, and predictive modeling of cellular responses.
