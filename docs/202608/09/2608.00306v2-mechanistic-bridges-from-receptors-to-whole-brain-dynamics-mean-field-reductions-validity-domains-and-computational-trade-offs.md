## 摘要
许多药理学和病理学扰动源于分子、突触或细胞尺度，但通过群体和全脑信号进行观察。跨尺度约化必须在保持可处理性的同时保留相关机制。本综述探讨约化后哪些微观机制仍然明确、可解释且可检验，以及这些模型支持何种论断。以基于主方程谱系、具有受体感知的自适应平均场作为实例，我们追踪有限大小群体统计量和半解析传递函数，将其引入通过连接组耦合的基于电导的自适应节点。我们将此策略与现象学神经质量模型、低维和群体密度约化、大规模尖峰模型以及学习或混合替代模型进行比较，涵盖计算工作量和内存流量。受体依赖性突触动力学、电导状态和尖峰频率适应性可以在跨尺度上保持可操作性，从而实现可解释的干预以及可检验的介观和宏观后果。然而，这依赖于粗粒化马尔可夫性、群体同质性、准平稳传递函数、矩闭合、区域均匀性以及特定测量的观测模型。一阶实现丢弃了协方差动力学，而宏观一致性无法确定唯一的分子原因。节点局部的生物学细节主要改变前置因子，而稠密的全局协方差则改变标度类。因此，跨尺度模型应根据其保留的干预和可观测量、有效性域、可辨识性、经验充分性和计算负担来评判。受体感知平均场并非普适，但在每一步约化均经过独立验证时，为选定的机制性问题提供了一种透明且可处理的策略。

## Abstract
Many pharmacological and pathological perturbations arise at molecular, synaptic, or cellular scales, but are observed through population and whole-brain signals. Cross-scale reductions must preserve relevant mechanisms while remaining tractable. This review asks which microscopic mechanisms remain explicit, interpretable, and testable after reduction, and what claims these models support. Using receptor-aware adaptive mean fields from the master-equation lineage as a worked case, we trace finite-size population statistics and semi-analytical transfer functions into conductance-based adaptive nodes coupled through the connectome. We compare this strategy with phenomenological neural masses, low-dimensional and population-density reductions, large-scale spiking models, and learned or hybrid surrogates, including computational work and memory traffic. Receptor-dependent synaptic kinetics, conductance state, and spike-frequency adaptation can remain manipulable across scales, enabling interpretable interventions and testable mesoscopic and macroscopic consequences. However, this relies on coarse-grained Markovianity, population homogeneity, quasi-stationary transfer functions, moment closure, regional uniformity, and measurement-specific observation models. First-order implementations discard covariance dynamics, while macroscopic agreement cannot identify a unique molecular cause. Node-local biological detail mainly changes prefactors, whereas dense global covariances change the scaling class. Cross-scale models should therefore be judged by the interventions and observables they preserve, validity domain, identifiability, empirical adequacy, and computational burden. Receptor-aware mean fields are not universal, but offer a transparent, tractable strategy for selected mechanistic questions when each reduction step is independently validated.
