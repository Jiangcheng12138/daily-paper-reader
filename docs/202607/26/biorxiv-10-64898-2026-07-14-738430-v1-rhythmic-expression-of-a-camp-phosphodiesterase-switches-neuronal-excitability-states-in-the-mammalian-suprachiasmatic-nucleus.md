## 摘要
神经元的转录组如何决定其电生理特性，以及这种特性在不同情境下如何变化，是神经生物学中的核心问题。哺乳动物视交叉上核（SCN）生物钟神经元内的细胞内分子钟通过转录-翻译反馈环驱动时钟基因表达的24小时节律。这些分子钟还控制SCN神经元放电的24小时节律——其中一部分神经元在中午前后进入静息去极化状态。我们利用单细胞RNA测序发现，Avp/Nms和Vip/Nms是发生日间静默的SCN神经元。我们还发现，在这些Nms阳性SCN神经元中，磷酸二酯酶Pde10a呈高水平和节律性表达。急性抑制Pde10a活性或提高细胞内cAMP水平（以绕过高Pde10a水平）会特异性地使这些Nms阳性SCN神经元提前数小时进入去极化静息状态。其他切换至静息去极化状态的神经元中可能也存在类似机制。

## Abstract
How the transcriptome of a neuron determines its electrophysiological properties and how this change in different contexts are major questions in neurobiology. The intracellular molecular clocks in the circadian pacemaker neurons in the mammalian suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) drive 24hr rhythms in clock gene expression via a transcriptional-translational feedback loop1,2. These molecular clocks also control 24hr rhythms in the firing of SCN neurons3-5 - a subset of which enter a silent depolarized state around midday6. We used single-cell RNA sequencing and identified that the Avp / Nms and Vip / Nms are the SCN neurons that undergo daytime silencing. We also found high level and rhythmic expression of the Pde10a phosphodiesterase in these Nms SCN neurons. Acutely inhibiting Pde10a activity or elevating intracellular cAMP - to bypass high Pde10a levels - specifically shifts these Nms+ SCN neurons into a depolarized silent state hours ahead of schedule. A similar mechanism may occur in other neurons that switch to silent depolarized states.
