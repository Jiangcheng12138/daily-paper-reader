## 摘要
我们基于Kuramoto模型提出一个由振荡器网络构成的细胞群体生物钟模型。振荡器之间的耦合由全局相互作用项描述，但我们引入了一种相位依赖的耦合机制，使得振荡器仅在对应生物钟周期特定阶段的时间窗口内相互作用。我们解析证明，在初始相位分布和耦合窗口长度满足特定条件时，该修正系统能够实现完全渐近相位同步。为连接这一理论框架与实验观测，我们引入基于小波分解的信号处理流程，从Per2::荧光素酶报告基因轨迹中提取定量振荡特征。随后，我们采用两步准蒙特卡洛滤波算法，基于野生型和Cry2敲除肝细胞球体数据集对模型进行校准。比较分析揭示了显著的表型差异，表明Cry2敲除条件改变了模型参数的可识别性景观，并引入了新的补偿机制，将初始群体异质性与长期宏观信号衰减混淆在一起。

## Abstract
We propose a model of the circadian clock in a population of cells based on a network of oscillators, derived from the Kuramoto model. The coupling between oscillators is described by a global interaction term but we introduce a phase-dependent coupling mechanism, such that oscillators interact only within a specific interval of the cycle corresponding to a specific stage of the circadian cycle. We analytically demonstrate that this modified system achieves complete asymptotic phase synchronisation, provided specific conditions on the initial phase distribution and the coupling window length are met. To bridge this theoretical framework with experimental observations, we introduce a signal processing procedure based on wavelet decomposition to extract quantitative oscillatory features from Per2::luciferase reporter traces. We then calibrate the model against datasets from both wild type and Cry2 knockout hepatocyte spheroids using a two-step quasi-Monte Carlo filtering algorithm. The comparative analysis reveals significant phenotypic divergence, showing that the Cry2KO condition alters the identifiability landscape of the model's parameters and introduces new compensatory mechanisms that confound the initial population heterogeneity with long-term macroscopic signal decay.
