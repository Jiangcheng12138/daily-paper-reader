## 摘要
食欲素（hypocretin）神经元（OXN）在前脑广泛传递与觉醒相关的信号，但它们的输入如何塑造动机行为仍知之甚少。上乳头体区域（SuM）是下丘脑后部的一个结构，与觉醒、主动应对和奖赏寻求相关，接收密集的食欲素神经支配。本文定义了上乳头体中的食欲素能终末（OXNSuM）如何将威胁显著性整合到接近动机过程中。通过病毒示踪和RNAscope，我们显示SuM神经元表达两种食欲素受体亚型，其中2型受体占主导。OXNSuM终末的光纤光度记录显示，足部电击和条件性威胁线索强烈激活该终末，对显著感觉刺激有适度反应，并在饮水期间显著抑制。OXNSuM终末的光遗传刺激强化了工具性反应，偏向实时位置偏好，并引发伏隔核中快速、刺激锁定的多巴胺释放。这些发现确定了一条从食欲素到SuM的通路，该通路在不确定性和威胁时被激活，但通过耦合到中脑边缘多巴胺促进接近行为，揭示了下丘脑觉醒信号与适应性动机反应相联系的机制。

## Abstract
Orexin (hypocretin) neurons (OXN) broadcast arousal related signals across the forebrain, yet how their inputs shape motivated behavior remains poorly understood. The supramammillary region (SuM), a posterior hypothalamic structure implicated in arousal, active coping and reward seeking, receives dense orexin innervation. Here, we define how orexinergic terminals in the SuM (OXNSuM) integrate threat salience with approach motivational processes. Using viral tracing and RNAscope, we show that SuM neurons express both orexin receptor subtypes, with type 2 receptors predominating. Fiber photometry recordings from OXNSuM terminals reveal strong activation by footshock and conditioned threat cues, modest responses to salient sensory stimuli, and pronounced suppression during water consumption. Optogenetic stimulation of OXNSuM terminals reinforces instrumental responding, biases real time place preference, and elicits rapid, stimulation locked dopamine release in the nucleus accumbens. These findings identify an orexin to SuM pathway that is engaged by uncertainty and threat yet promotes approach behavior through coupling to mesolimbic dopamine, revealing a mechanism linking hypothalamic arousal signals to adaptive motivational responses.
